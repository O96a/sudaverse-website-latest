Sudanese AI professionals building products.

Sudaverse is an applied AI and digital systems company, Sudanese-founded and globally connected.

Who we are

We are engineers, researchers and advisors who build production-oriented AI products and digital infrastructure. The team spans machine learning, software engineering, cybersecurity, applied research, consulting and business development.

Our work is one body of work, from language technology to secure systems, not a set of unrelated business units.

Vision and mission

Sudaverse is an integrated technology ecosystem that combines applied AI and cybersecurity to establish a sovereign, secure and customized digital infrastructure. It brings together the development of language models on one side, and the security of data, infrastructure and digital systems on the other, in order to provide a trusted and protected operating environment.

Vision
Build national capabilities entirely through Sudanese expertise, so that Sudan becomes a producer of ethical and inclusive artificial intelligence rather than merely a consumer of ready-made tools.
Mission
Empower institutions and developers by building customized and trusted AI and cybersecurity systems and tools, supported by advanced defensive and security solutions to protect data and safely bridge the digital divide.

Training and consulting

Sudaverse offers training and consulting to organizations, academic institutions and students. Training builds practical skills in applied AI, data and cybersecurity, in levels from foundations to leadership. Consulting helps an organization define and plan its work before anything is built. Content and duration are agreed with each institution.

We welcome cooperation with universities and academic institutions on curricula, student training and applied research. An institution that wants to work with us can start with a conversation.

Why Sudan matters technically

The language and the deployment conditions are engineering constraints. They shape what we build and how we test it.

Low-resource dialects
Sudanese Arabic dialects are a low-resource language variant. Tokenizers, text normalization and training corpora have to be measured and built for them, which is the work behind our open tools.
Code-switching
Everyday text moves between Arabic, English and local languages, often within a single message.
Informal writing
Informal writing is the norm in everyday text. A system has to expect dialect spelling and informal phrasing rather than assume clean, standard input.
Local and private deployment
Some organizations need sensitive data and models to stay inside their own environment. Selected Sudaverse systems can run against locally hosted models.

Values

  • Cultural integrity

    Our products speak the way people speak. Local language, context and heritage are design inputs, not afterthoughts.

  • Ethical AI

    Bias, fairness and privacy are engineering concerns. We evaluate what we build for the contexts it serves.

  • Open collaboration

    We publish tools and benchmarks on GitHub, and we welcome researchers and institutions who want to build with us.

  • Education first

    Teaching and learning come first. SudaTutor is free for every Sudanese student, teacher and educator.
