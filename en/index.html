Research & Engineering

What we do

From infrastructure to applied AI, Sudaverse works with institutions and companies along the whole path. Every engagement starts with a conversation about the problem.

Discuss your needs
  • Infrastructure setupNetworks, servers and cloud or on-premises environments, planned, built and documented, ready to host data and AI workloads securely.
  • AI adoptionFrom choosing the right use case to running Arabic-capable models inside your own environment and connecting them to daily work.
  • CybersecuritySecurity assessments, hardening and monitoring for networks and systems, including the models and data behind AI applications.
  • IT consultingIndependent advice on digital strategy, architecture and technology choices, before money is spent and before anything is built.
  • Training and capacity buildingPractical courses in applied AI, data and cybersecurity for teams, institutions and students, in levels from foundations to leadership.
  • Data engineering and analyticsPipelines, databases and dashboards that turn scattered records, Arabic text included, into information teams can act on.

Projects

Every project below is live today.

SudaTutor

Live

Learn the Sudanese curriculum with AI assistance.

An intelligent educational platform that helps learners study Sudan’s national curriculum in an interactive, clear and easy-to-understand way. It is supported by AI that adapts to the learner’s level and gives explanations appropriate to the student’s age.

Built for Learners studying the Sudanese national curriculum.

Terab

Live

Arabic-first agriculture platform.

An integrated agriculture platform powered by AI that gives Sudanese farmers access to specialized agricultural advice and helps them monitor pests, rainfall and irrigation, to raise productivity and reduce risk.

Built for Farmers and agricultural teams working in Sudan.

Sudan Monitor

Live

Geospatial situational-awareness platform.

Sudan Monitor brings information onto a map so teams can follow what is happening, and where.

Built for Organizations and teams that follow critical situations in conflict areas.

SudaNDR

Live

AI-assisted network detection and response.

A smart cyber-defense platform that detects attacks and protects network traffic. Its AI engine analyzes network traffic in real time and recommends immediate protection measures.

Built for Security and IT teams responsible for defending networks.

From raw text to a deployed model

The pipeline behind Sudaverse language projects, from collection to deployment.

  1. Collect

    Gather raw Sudanese Arabic text from many sources.

  2. Clean and normalize

    Standardize spelling, dialect variation and formatting.

    View on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

  3. Refine

    Repair sentences and score quality at corpus scale.

    LLMCorpusKit

  4. Train

    Fine-tune language models on the curated corpus.

  5. Evaluate

    Measure how well tokenizers and models handle Sudanese dialects.

    View on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

  6. Deploy

    Ship products that run on the models, starting with SudaTutor.
