What we do

One engineering team, from infrastructure to applied AI. Every engagement starts with a conversation about your problem.

  • Infrastructure setupNetworks, servers and cloud or on-premises environments, built, secured and documented.
  • AI adoptionFrom the right use case to Arabic-capable models running inside your own environment.
  • CybersecurityAssessments, hardening and monitoring for systems, data and AI models.
  • IT consultingIndependent advice on strategy, architecture and technology, before you spend.
  • TrainingPractical courses in applied AI, data and cybersecurity for teams and students.
  • Data and analyticsPipelines, databases and dashboards that turn scattered records into answers.
