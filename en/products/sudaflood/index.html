SudaFlood

Live

Flood monitoring and early warning.

An early-warning platform that helps monitor floods and torrents in Sudan interactively. Its AI analyzes satellite data and sends safety alerts directly to citizens.

Request a briefing

Built for

Organizations and communities exposed to flood risk.

Capabilities

  • Hydrological forecasting: predicts the path and rise of water levels ahead of time.
  • Coverage of Sudan: focuses on river courses and critical hazard areas.
  • Shelter guidance and alerts: identifies the nearest safe areas and issues evacuation alerts.
  • Alerts can be extended to local languages.

Status

SudaFlood is live.
