Projects

Sudaverse builds AI projects and open tools around Arabic language, local context and the workflows of specific domains.

Every project page follows the same path: the problem, how the project tackles it, how it works, and how to get involved.

AI Products

AI products for education and agriculture, built to work in Arabic.

  • SudaTutor

    Learn the Sudanese curriculum with AI assistance.

    Built for Learners studying the Sudanese national curriculum.

  • Terab

    Arabic-first agriculture platform.

    Built for Farmers and agricultural teams working in Sudan.

  • NodeGrid

    Self-hosted control plane for AI agents.

    Built for Organizations and engineering teams that run AI agents on their own infrastructure.

Data & Insights

Geospatial platforms for situational awareness and flood early warning.

  • Sudan Monitor

    Geospatial situational-awareness platform.

    Built for Organizations and teams that follow critical situations in conflict areas.

  • FloodWatch

    Flood monitoring and early warning.

    Built for Organizations and communities exposed to flood risk.

  • Sudata

    Sudanese knowledge atlas.

    Built for Researchers and readers looking for information about Sudan.

Secure AI Systems

Security systems that apply AI to defending networks.

  • AegisNDR

    AI-assisted network detection and response.

    Built for Security and IT teams responsible for defending networks.

Research & Language Technology

Open tooling for Arabic language models and their training data.

  • URRI

    Sudanese language models.

    Built for People and teams who work in Sudanese Arabic.

  • Sudanizer

    Tokenization engine for Sudanese dialects.

    Built for Researchers and teams working with Sudanese dialect text.

  • LLMCorpusKit

    Corpus refinery for large-scale Arabic language-model training data.

    Built for Teams building or fine-tuning Arabic language models.
