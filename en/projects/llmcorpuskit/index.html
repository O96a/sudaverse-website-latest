LLMCorpusKit

Corpus refinery for large-scale Arabic language-model training data.

LLMCorpusKit cleans and polishes large Arabic corpora and uses AI-powered semantic repair to fix sentences and improve quality, so the refined corpus stays coherent and culturally authentic.

Get involvedView on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

The problem

The problem

Large Arabic corpora collected for training are noisy: broken sentences, inconsistent spelling and fragments lower the quality of any model trained on them.

How LLMCorpusKit tackles it

LLMCorpusKit refines a corpus in stages, from surface normalization to deep semantic analysis. It repairs sentences with AI and standardizes spelling while keeping dialect vocabulary.

  • Multi-stage cleaning from surface normalization to deep semantic analysis.
  • AI-powered sentence repair for grammatical errors and fragmented text.
  • Keeps dialect expressions and vocabulary while standardizing orthography. It does not translate to Modern Standard Arabic.
  • Processes large corpora in chunks, resumes after an interruption and shows live progress.

How it works

The path a user takes through LLMCorpusKit, step by step.

  1. Load

    Point the kit at a large Arabic corpus.

  2. Clean

    Multi-stage cleaning, from normalization to semantic analysis.

  3. Repair

    AI repairs grammatical errors and fragmented sentences.

  4. Standardize

    Spelling is standardized, dialect expressions are kept.

  5. Process

    Chunks run with live progress and resume after an interruption.

Research behind it:Arabic NLP, with a focus on Sudanese dialects
