Sudan Monitor

Live

Geospatial situational-awareness platform.

Sudan Monitor brings information onto a map so teams can follow what is happening, and where.

Get involved

The problem

The problem

Teams following critical situations in conflict areas piece together information from many scattered sources, with no single view of what is happening, and where.

How Sudan Monitor tackles it

Sudan Monitor ingests information live from multiple sources, news and television included, and places it on a multi-layer, Sudan-centred map beside critical infrastructure.

  • A Sudan-centred map with multiple layers.
  • Monitoring of critical infrastructure in Sudan.
  • Live ingestion of information from multiple sources.
  • Live news and television monitoring.

How it works

The path a user takes through Sudan Monitor, step by step.

  1. Ingest

    Information arrives live from multiple sources.

  2. Watch

    News and television are followed as they broadcast.

  3. Map

    Each item lands on a Sudan-centred map.

  4. Layer

    Switch layers, critical infrastructure included.

  5. Follow

    The team sees what is happening, and where.
