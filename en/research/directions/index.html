Research directions

Six directions, chosen because our projects depend on the answers. The map shows which projects each direction feeds.

Arabic NLP, with a focus on Sudanese dialects

Language technology for the Arabic people actually write and speak in Sudan: dialect vocabulary, informal spelling and code-switching, in a low-resource setting.

Topics

  • Dialect-specific tokenization
  • Cleaning and normalizing dialect corpora
  • Sudanese language models that run on phones and tablets
  • Code-switching between Arabic, English and local languages

Feeds these projects

  • URRI
  • Sudanizer
  • LLMCorpusKit

Papers

  • Sudanizer: A Dialect-Specific Byte-Pair Encoding Tokenizer for NLP ArabicIEEE FITAT 2026

Artifacts

  • Tokenizing Sudanese Arabic: what a public benchmark shows

Adversarial cyberattack prevention

Detecting and stopping attacks on networks and on AI systems themselves, from abnormal traffic to attempts to mislead or manipulate a model.

Topics

  • AI-assisted detection of abnormal network behavior
  • Robustness of models against adversarial inputs
  • Threats to AI in critical sectors such as maritime
  • Security requirements for locally deployed models

Feeds these projects

  • AegisNDR

Papers

  • AI Cybersecurity in the Maritime Industry: A Comprehensive Review of Threats, Regulations, and Defensive StrategiesIEEE FITAT 2026

AI cognitive architectures

How AI agents perceive, remember, plan and act over long tasks, and how to build agentic systems that stay reliable and inspectable.

Topics

  • Memory and planning in agentic systems
  • Tool use and multi-step reasoning
  • Evaluating agent behavior on long tasks
  • Grounding agents in Arabic-language context

Feeds these projects

Exploratory: not yet tied to a project.

Public sector infrastructure adaptation

Adapting digital infrastructure and AI to the constraints of public institutions: limited connectivity, sensitive data that must stay in the country, and systems that have to keep running.

Topics

  • Local and private deployment of AI for institutions
  • Bilingual public knowledge bases
  • Monitoring critical infrastructure
  • Resilient systems for low-connectivity settings

Feeds these projects

  • Sudata
  • Sudan Monitor

Geospatial and climate intelligence

Turning satellite imagery and live information into early warnings and decisions on the ground: floods, crops and critical sites.

Topics

  • Flood forecasting and early warning
  • Crop health from satellite imagery
  • Weather-aware irrigation planning
  • Multi-layer situational maps

Feeds these projects

  • FloodWatch
  • Terab
  • Sudan Monitor

Adaptive learning for low-resource settings

Education technology that adapts to each learner’s level, follows the national curriculum and works where connectivity and devices are limited.

Topics

  • Explanations adapted to age and level
  • Curriculum-aligned content in Arabic
  • Progress tracking and assessment
  • Learning over low-bandwidth connections

Feeds these projects

  • SudaTutor
