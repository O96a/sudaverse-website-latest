Research and development

Research that becomes infrastructure. We choose our directions because our products depend on the answers, and we publish the tools that come out of the work.

Research directions

These are directions we are investigating. None of them has been published yet.

  • Natural language processing for Sudanese Arabic

    Language technology that captures the richness of Sudanese dialects and the low-resource conditions they live in.

    Topics under investigation

    • Dialect recognition across Sudanese Arabic varieties
    • Code-switching between Arabic, English and local languages
    • Low-resource language modeling

  • Ethical AI and bias mitigation

    Measuring and reducing cultural bias, with evaluation suited to African contexts.

    Topics under investigation

    • Cultural bias detection in language models
    • Fairness metrics for African contexts
    • Privacy preservation in data collection

  • Computer vision for heritage material

    Digitizing and analyzing documents, archives and visual heritage.

    Topics under investigation

    • Document restoration for historical manuscripts
    • Pattern recognition for cultural motifs
    • Image enhancement for archived photographs

  • Applied AI for health, education and agriculture

    Research that feeds the products: tutoring, crop and farm intelligence, and health information.

    Topics under investigation

    • Adaptive tutoring in Sudanese Arabic
    • Crop and farm intelligence
    • Multilingual health information

Applied AI for health, education and agriculture

Health is one of the directions we are exploring, next to education and agriculture, where our products are already live. It is a direction under investigation, and nothing in it has been published yet.

How research becomes products

Research at Sudaverse is not a separate department. The directions are the ones our products depend on, and the products are built on what the work shows.

Language
Work on dialects, code-switching and low-resource modeling is aimed at products that understand and answer in Sudanese Arabic, such as SudaTutor.
Applied AI
Applied directions feed tutoring in SudaTutor and crop and farm intelligence in Terab.
Evaluation
Bias detection, fairness metrics for African contexts and privacy preservation shape how we evaluate what we build.

Published research

Where the work is useful beyond our own products, we publish it. Our open tools and benchmarks are public on GitHub, and contributions are welcome.
