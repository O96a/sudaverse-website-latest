AI knowledge systems (RAG)

Institutions hold decades of documents that nobody can search or query in Arabic.

What we build toward it

Arabic-first assistants and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems built on an institution’s own documents and data, with deployment options that keep that data in its control.
