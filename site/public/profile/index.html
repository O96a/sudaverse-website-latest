02

Global AI tools are not built around Sudan.

Language

Sudanese Arabic is a low-resource dialect. General-purpose models and tokenizers break its words apart and miss its vocabulary.

Context

Curricula, admission policies and institutions are local. Tools designed elsewhere do not follow them.

Control

Institutions need AI they can run in their own environment, with decisions they can explain and audit.

Sudaverse builds the missing layer: Arabic-first products that follow local rules and can run where the data lives.

Operators

A browser dashboard: agents, chat, pipelines, workflows, usage and alerts.

Sudaverse | SmartSelect

10

From application to approved decision

Eligibility

A rules engine checks each application against policy. Every rule cites its source.

Scorecard

The official score: weighted criteria, out of 100.

Fuzzy advice

A recommendation band and the rules that fired. Advisory only.

Ranking

The official score orders the cohort; capacity sets the cut-off.

Governance

Panel decision, sign-offs and an approval chain with separation of duties.

Two tracks, kept apart on purpose.

The scorecard is the number the panel signs and publishes. The fuzzy layer never changes it, and when the two disagree beyond a set threshold, the file goes to manual review instead of being quietly resolved.
